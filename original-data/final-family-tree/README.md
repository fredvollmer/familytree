# Complete Family Tree of Fredric Muller Vollmer

This is the canonical local package aggregating every Family Tree project chat through 2 September 2026. The GEDCOM is the standardized tree source of truth; the JSON and workbook are synchronized review formats.

## Canonical files

- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree.ged` — GEDCOM 5.5.1 source-of-truth tree.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Canonical_Data.json` — complete machine-readable people, families, sources, corrections, and cross-chat provenance.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Index.xlsx` — synchronized review workbook; its eight recovered tabs are retained for provenance and seven consolidated tabs reflect the current tree.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_People.csv` and `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Families.csv` — flat audit tables.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Sources.md` — all recovered and later source ledgers in one file.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Source_Inventory.csv` — source and preserved-record inventory with SHA-256 hashes.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Vital_Date_Coverage.csv` — one-row-per-person birth/death coverage and explicit unresolved/privacy outcomes.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Vital_Dates_Research.csv` — accepted additions, rejected candidates, source quality, and conflicts from the vital-date pass.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Occupation_Coverage.csv` — one-row-per-person occupation/role coverage with explicit unresolved and privacy outcomes.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Occupation_Research.csv` — accepted occupational facts, rejected candidates, dates, categories, transcription controls, and row-level citations.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Media_Audit.json` and `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Media_Inventory.csv` — one-row-per-person photo availability, exact person-to-evidence mappings, external image-page links, and rights/access notes.
- `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_Merge_Audit.md` and `Fredric_Vollmer_Complete_Family_Tree_VALIDATION.txt` — merge and integrity checks.
- `records/` — preserved source images and certificates copied from the later records-first tasks.
- `record-previews/` — 18 rendered page images exposing the relevant pages of the nine preserved evidence PDFs without replacing the original files.
- The recovered records-first maternal package remains alongside these files for provenance.

The portable ZIP includes the canonical tree, ledgers, workbook, research notes, and every preserved source record. Reproducible preview images and the earlier historical report remain in the repository but are omitted from the ZIP to keep it below GitHub's single-file limit.

## Privacy and relationship controls

Living details are minimized. Jan is recorded as Fredric's biological mother and Chris Vollmer's stepmother. Henry Richard Vollmer and Mary Alice Thoren are Chris's biological parents; Chris is Fredric's paternal half-brother. Peter Vollmer is recorded as Chris's owner-confirmed brother and Henry's obituary-supported son without inferring Peter's mother. The 1950 census identifies William John Thoren and Alice Gallaher Thoren as Mary Alice's parents. U.S. and Swedish records extend William's ancestry through Christian Andrew Thoren and Augusta Nilsdotter into eighteenth-century Sweden. Alice Gallaher's parents remain unresolved and no member-tree hint was imported as fact.

The media audit covers all 391 canonical people. A portrait is displayed only when a verified, reusable file is archived. External or account-gated photographs remain linked with provenance rather than copied, and portraits for people explicitly marked living remain withheld. Preserved evidence is attached through explicit IDs instead of name/filename guessing; rejected same-name records remain visible as exclusion controls.
