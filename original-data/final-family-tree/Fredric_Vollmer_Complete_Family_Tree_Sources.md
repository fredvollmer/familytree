# Consolidated source ledger

This ledger aggregates the recovered maternal canonical package, the later Vollmer-Marsh direct-ancestor research, the extended-family research, the Mary Alice Thoren ancestry investigation, owner corrections, the vital-date enrichment pass, and the occupation/role enrichment pass. The GEDCOM uses S1-S48; the detailed local ledgers below retain their original identifiers.

## Cross-chat provenance

- **Build Family Tree** — recovered canonical 169-person maternal direct tree, corrected maternal collateral households, GEDCOM, workbook, report, chart, source key, and validation package.
- **Continue Vollmer Family Tree** — established the paternal research scope and Charles Frederic Vollmer / Doris Marsh anchors; superseded where later records proved more detail.
- **Research Vollmer-Marsh ancestry** and **Continue Vollmer-Marsh research** — records-first paternal direct ancestry, preserved originals, conflicts, and rejected candidates.
- **Extend paternal family tree** — three collateral rings on both sides, owner relationship corrections, record audits, and rejected same-name candidates.

- **Trace Mary Alice Thoren ancestry** — Ancestry.com census, vital, Swedish church, and household-survey records extending the proven Thoren line into eighteenth-century Sweden.

- **Vital-date enrichment** — audited every canonical person, restored dropped project facts, added supported dates or conservative ranges, and classified every remaining gap as unresolved or privacy-limited.
- **Occupation and role enrichment** — reviewed Ancestry records in the signed-in browser, reconciled results to canonical people, preserved dated occupational changes, and classified every person without a supported fact.
- **Photo and evidence media audit** — audited all 391 canonical people for verified portrait availability, linked external photo pages without copying restricted files, explicitly mapped all preserved record media to people, rendered relevant PDF pages, and retained identity exclusions.

## Extended-family ledger

# Extended-family source catalog

## Scope

This catalog supports the collateral-family expansion of the existing Vollmer and McCardle Ancestry trees. It covers the owner's, parents', and grandparents' aunts/uncles and first cousins on both sides. It does not extend a cousin into a spouse or descendant, except that a spouse or partner may be recorded when needed to show a cousin's parentage. Sibling groups earlier than the great-grandparent generation are out of scope.

## Evidence and privacy rules

- Prefer owner-supplied facts for living people and keep their profiles private.
- Prefer original censuses, civil vital records, obituaries, directories, military records, probate, deeds, and church registers for deceased people.
- Treat Ancestry hints, member trees, Find a Grave, compiled genealogies, social media, and potential-parent cards as leads rather than proof.
- Record full/half/adoptive/step status explicitly and do not infer shared parentage from a household alone.
- Preserve conflicts, negative searches, and same-name exclusions.

## Existing direct-line evidence

The paternal anchor identities, conflicts, citations, and preserved records remain in `Vollmer-Marsh-sources.md`. Those citations may be cross-referenced here without duplicating the direct-ancestor people file.

## Proven Thoren ancestry

The direct record chain now extends Mary Alice's Thoren side through several Swedish generations:

1. **William John Thoren**, born 19 July 1901 at Great Falls, Montana; died 26 October 1991 in Washington. The 1910 Great Falls census places him as son of Christian and Augusta Thoren. His World War II draft registration supplies the exact birth date and place.
2. **Christian Andrew Thoren**, born 27 January 1861 at Ignaberga, Kristianstad, Sweden; died 9 May 1921 at Great Falls, Montana. Swedish birth and household records identify his parents as **Anders Troedsson** and **Kjersti Månsdotter**.
3. **Augusta Nilsdotter Thoren**, born 30 November 1860 at Hjärnarp, Kristianstad, Sweden; died 4 December 1913 at Great Falls. Her Swedish birth, baptism, and childhood household identify **Nils Svensson** and **Christina Persdotter** as her parents.
4. **Anders Troedsson**, born 2 May 1824 at Norra Sandby, Kristianstad, appears first as son of **Trued Andersson** and **Bortha Nilsdotter**, then as husband of Kjersti and father of Christian in linked household surveys.
5. **Kjersti Månsdotter**, born 31 October 1825 at Mellby, Kristianstad, is established as Christian's mother by the household survey. No parent-naming baptism was located, so her line stops there.
6. **Trued Andersson**, born 25 April 1792 at Norra Sandby, and **Bortha Nilsdotter**, born in 1783 at Gumlösa, are Anders's parents. No proved parent generation was found for either.
7. **Nils Svensson**, born 3 May 1825 at Ränneslöv, Halland, married **Christina Persdotter** on 26 October 1850 at Ränneslöv. His baptism names only his mother, **Gunnilla Nilsdotter**. No father is inferred from the Svensson patronymic.
8. **Gunnilla Nilsdotter**, born 20 September 1796 at Ränneslöv, was baptized as daughter of **Nils Johansson** and **Elna Nilsdotter**. Their own identities and dates remain unproved.
9. **Christina Persdotter**, born 24 January 1823 at Hasslöv, Halland, was baptized as daughter of **Pehr Erlandsson** and **Ingier Hansdotter**.

The earliest dated direct ancestor reached is Bortha Nilsdotter, born in 1783. The tree also reaches six named but undated ancestors one generation earlier: Nils Johansson and Elna Nilsdotter; Erland Pålsson and Bengta Pehrsdotter; and Hans Månsson and Maria Hansdotter.
