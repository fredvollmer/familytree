# Wallace Ray Fischer direct-ancestry research log

Researched in the signed-in Ancestry session on 2 September 2026 after the owner's correction. Scope was limited to Wallace Ray Fischer's parents and their direct ancestors; no collateral relatives were added.

## Outcome

- Wallace's parents are Raymond Charles Fischer Jr. (22 Nov 1934–22 Sep 2022) and Wanda June Wallace Fischer.
- Wanda and June are the same person: Texas marriage records use Wanda J. Wallace and Wanda June Wallace, while the 2022 tribute uses June.
- Raymond's paternal line reaches Johann Daniel Fischer of Grossaspach; German church records name the immigrant's parents.
- Wanda's Wallace/Highley line reaches Daniel Franklin McCoy and the memorial-supported James and Mary Highley stopping point.
- Twenty-one full-resolution images explicitly labeled Photo type: Grave were preserved with memorial URLs and contributor attribution.
