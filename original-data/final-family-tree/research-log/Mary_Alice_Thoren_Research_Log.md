# Mary Alice Thoren ancestry research log

Updated: 2 September 2026

## Research standard

The GitHub family-tree package is the source of truth. Ancestry.com was used through the signed-in in-app browser as a record discovery and image/index access layer, not as an authority by itself. Parent links were imported only when a parent-naming record or a coherent household chain established the relationship. Public member trees and Ancestry suggested records were treated as leads and were rejected when their identity or relationship could not be proved.

Living-person details remain minimized. Mary Alice's exact birth date is not included in the public canonical tree.

## Mary Alice and her parents

- The family owner confirmed that Mary Alice was born in Port Townsend, Washington; she was Henry Richard Vollmer's first wife, before Jan Muller Vollmer; and she was Chris Vollmer's biological mother.
- The Washington State Archives records the marriage of **Henry R. Vollmer** and **Mary A. Thoren** on **16 September 1955** in King County. Reference `kingcoarchmc207309`: https://digitalarchives.wa.gov/Record/View/437A4FBD9EF3DA4BD90C60795808BC69
- The 1950 U.S. census records **William J. Thoren**, age 48 and born in Montana; wife **Alice**, age 46 and born in Alaska; and daughter **Mary Alice**, age 14 and born in Washington, in one Seattle household. Seattle, King County, Washington, ED 40-124, sheet 74, lines 23-25; NARA image `43290879-Washington-031283-0021`: https://1950census.archives.gov/search/?ed=40-124&state=WA&page=1
- The Washington State Archives records the marriage of **William J. Thoren** and **Alice Gallaher** on **16 November 1929** in Pierce County. This establishes Alice's maiden surname. Reference `prcmc-v23-00709`: https://digitalarchives.wa.gov/Record/View/33AF9C076D80A9452C66FD351D17CD73

Together, the census household and William–Alice marriage record establish William John “Bill” Thoren and Alice Gallaher Thoren as Mary Alice's parents.

## Proven Thoren ancestry

The direct record chain now extends Mary Alice's Thoren side through several Swedish generations:

1. **William John Thoren**, born 19 July 1901 at Great Falls, Montana; died 26 October 1991 in Washington. The 1910 Great Falls census places him as son of Christian and Augusta Thoren. His World War II draft registration supplies the exact birth date and place.
2. **Christian Andrew Thoren**, born 27 January 1861 at Ignaberga, Kristianstad, Sweden; died 9 May 1921 at Great Falls, Montana. Swedish birth and household records identify his parents as **Anders Troedsson** and **Kjersti Månsdotter**.
3. **Augusta Nilsdotter Thoren**, born 30 November 1860 at Hjärnarp, Kristianstad, Sweden; died 4 December 1913 at Great Falls. Her Swedish birth, baptism, and childhood household identify **Nils Svensson** and **Christina Persdotter** as her parents.
4. **Anders Troedsson**, born 2 May 1824 at Norra Sandby, Kristianstad, appears first as son of **Trued Andersson** and **Bortha Nilsdotter**, then as husband of Kjersti and father of Christian in linked household surveys.
5. **Kjersti Månsdotter**, born 31 October 1825 at Mellby, Kristianstad, is established as Christian's mother by the household survey. No parent-naming baptism was located, so her line stops there.
6. **Trued Andersson**, born 25 April 1792 at Norra Sandby, and **Bortha Nilsdotter**, born in 1783 at Gumlösa, are Anders's parents. No proved parent generation was found for either.
7. **Nils Svensson**, born 3 May 1825 at Ränneslöv, Halland, married **Christina Persdotter** on 26 October 1850 at Ränneslöv. His baptism names only his mother, **Gunnilla Nilsdotter**. No father is inferred from the Svensson patronymic.
8. **Gunnilla Nilsdotter**, born 20 September 1796 at Ränneslöv, was baptized as daughter of **Nils Johansson** and **Elna Nilsdotter**. Their own identities and dates remain unproved.
9. **Christina Persdotter**, born 24 January 1823 at Hasslöv, Halland, was baptized as daughter of **Pehr Erlandsson** and **Ingier Hansdotter**.
10. **Pehr Erlandsson**, born 10 February 1790 at Hishult, Halland, was baptized as son of **Erland Pålsson** and **Bengta Pehrsdotter**. Their names are proved, but a reliable record linking either to an earlier parent generation was not found.
11. **Ingier Hansdotter**, born 20 March 1794 at Hasslöv, was baptized as daughter of **Hans Månsson** and **Maria Hansdotter**. Their names are proved; no earlier link was imported.

The earliest dated direct ancestor reached is Bortha Nilsdotter, born in 1783. The tree also reaches six named but undated ancestors one generation earlier: Nils Johansson and Elna Nilsdotter; Erland Pålsson and Bengta Pehrsdotter; and Hans Månsson and Maria Hansdotter.

## Conflicts retained

- Christian's 1900 census estimate is January 1862; his Swedish birth is 27 January 1861. The Swedish birth controls.
- Augusta's 1900 census estimate is November 1861; her Swedish birth and baptism show 30 November 1860. The Swedish records control.
- Pehr's household survey reports 10 February 1791; his baptism records birth on 10 February 1790. The baptism controls.
- Christian's Montana death index misplaces wife Augusta Nelson in the mother field. Swedish records identifying Anders Troedsson and Kjersti Månsdotter control the parent link.
- Augusta's surname appears as Nilsdotter in the household, Svensson in the baptism index, and Nilson/Nelson in U.S. records. These are retained as variants of one coherently linked person.

## Rejected and unresolved leads

- An unsourced public member tree names **James M. Gallaher** and **Agnes Cope** as Alice's parents: https://www.ancestry.com/family-tree/person/tree/25021352/person/142195452883. No source on that tree or independent parent-naming record connected them to Alice. They were not imported.
- A 1910 Fort Gibbon, Alaska, record for James M. Gallaher is not sufficient to establish him as Alice's father and lists him as single: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/7884/records/192848833
- Exact 1910 and 1920 census searches did not locate Alice in a proved Gallaher or Cope household. Alice Wicks and other Alaska-born matches were unrelated.
- The Clarence A. Thoren–Millie Anderson household in Jefferson County lacks a connection to William and remains rejected.
- Augusta Paulina, born 20 July 1860 at Tunge, Älvsborg, is a different person despite similar parent names: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/9731/records/20378352
- A Pår household in Björnlunda, Södermanland, has the wrong patronymic and locality and is not Pehr Erlandsson: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/9731/records/115986975
- Troed Ohlasson's Hjärnarp baptism has the wrong patronymic and does not identify Trued Andersson: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/60361/records/21624480
- A Bengta Persdotter born in Knäred later married Jons Larsson and is not proved to be Pehr's mother. The similar baptism and marriage were not used: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/61619/records/356972 and https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/60363/records/2295516
- A possible Erland Pålsson baptism on 29 July 1756 at Trälshult names Pål Jönsson and Elna Jönsdotter, but no reviewed record closely links that infant to Pehr's father. It remains a lead, not a canonical parent link: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/61619/records/711356
