# Extended-family research report

Research opened: 1 September 2026

## Scope

Build a local extended-family research package, using the two existing Ancestry trees only as read-only evidence sources:

1. Owner's collateral ring: each parent's siblings or half-siblings and those siblings' children.
2. Parents' collateral ring: each grandparent's siblings or half-siblings and those siblings' children.
3. Grandparents' collateral ring: each great-grandparent's siblings or half-siblings and those siblings' children.

Stop at first cousins for each focus person. Do not develop a cousin's spouse, children, or later descendants. Add a spouse or partner only when needed to represent a cousin's parentage. Do not expand sibling groups earlier than the great-grandparent generation.
