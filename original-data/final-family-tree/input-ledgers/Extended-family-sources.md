# Extended-family source catalog

## Scope

This catalog supports the collateral-family expansion of the existing Vollmer and McCardle Ancestry trees. It covers the owner's, parents', and grandparents' aunts/uncles and first cousins on both sides. It does not extend a cousin into a spouse or descendant, except that a spouse or partner may be recorded when needed to show a cousin's parentage. Sibling groups earlier than the great-grandparent generation are out of scope.

## Evidence and privacy rules

- Prefer owner-supplied facts for living people and keep their profiles private.
- Prefer original censuses, civil vital records, obituaries, directories, military records, probate, deeds, and church registers for deceased people.
- Treat Ancestry hints, member trees, Find a Grave, compiled genealogies, social media, and potential-parent cards as leads rather than proof.
- Record full/half/adoptive/step status explicitly and do not infer shared parentage from a household alone.
- Preserve conflicts, negative searches, and same-name exclusions.

## Existing direct-line evidence

The paternal anchor identities, conflicts, citations, and preserved records remain in `Vollmer-Marsh-sources.md`. Those citations may be cross-referenced here without duplicating the direct-ancestor people file.
